# β1

## Chinese

β1是一个早期的版本，相较于上一版本*Demo*，

### β1**更新了**：

1.**选择点的功能**：能够通过按下`H+O`切换到点的位置；

2.**重置了旋转的功能**：现在，`C+S`的旋转功能改为了指定度数旋转，而非Demo中按下`C+S`旋转一度；

3.**更改了光标的颜色**：现在，光标是红色的圆球；

### β1**修复了**：

1.**当作过一条线再移动点时，仍会作出线的bug**：按下`C+其他键`之后不再会出现一条线了；

2.**移动时颜色特效不生效的bug**：现在，在作线时，光标会改变颜色；

## English

β1 is an early version. Compared with the previous version, *Demo*,

### Updates in β1:

1. **Point selection function**: You can switch to a point’s position by pressing `H+O`.
   
2. **Revamped rotation function**: The `C+S` shortcut now rotates by a specified angle, instead of rotating by one degree as it did in Demo.
   
3. **Changed cursor color**: The cursor is now a red sphere.

### Fixes in β1:

1. **Bug where lines kept being drawn when moving points after creating a line**: No extra lines will be generated after pressing `C` combined with other keys.
   
2. **Bug where color effects failed to activate during movement**: The cursor now changes color while drawing lines.
